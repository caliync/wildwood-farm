Welcome to Wildwood Farm

A cozy forest where you befriend animals instead of catching them. Friends move into your barn and leave you gifts.

  • 🚶Walk with WASD or arrow keys, or tap the ground. Hold Shift to sprint (it uses stamina). Drag to turn the camera, scroll to zoom.
  • 🥕Pick a food on your hotbar (1–9), walk up to an animal and press Offer (E).
  • 💗Stay still while its heart fills. When the ring shrinks onto the circle and turns green, press Space or tap the animal.
  • 🌱Grow taming food. Pick a seed in your Bag (B), then plant it in your field. Rain makes crops grow twice as fast. Acorns grow wild in the grove.
  • 🪙Sell animal drops to the merchant: each barn animal leaves its own gift. Buy seeds and snacks at the farm shop. Your bag holds 100 items at first, and you can upgrade it.
  • 📜Daily quests are pinned on the board by the well. Pet and feed your barn animals to raise their hearts for better gifts. Watch for the Spirit Fawn in the rain and the Alpha Wolf on full-moon nights.
  • ⭐Level up by taming, fighting and farming. Spend skill points in the skill tree (K) on speed, stamina, strength, hearts, luck and crops.
  • 🤗Care for your animals: walk near them and tap the Pet tag above their heads. They get lonely over time. Pet or feed each one up to 3 times a day to keep its care up. Happy animals leave gifts faster; a lonely one (💔) stops leaving gifts until you visit.
  • ✋Switch what's in your hand with 1–9 or by tapping the hotbar. Hold food to offer it, a seed to plant it, or a weapon to fight (press its number again to use it). X empties your hands.
  • 🌫️Weather brings its own enemies: 🌧️ rain brings Mud Boars, 🌫️ fog brings Ghost Wolves and ⛈️ storms bring Storm Wolves. They drop extra forge materials.
  • 🏚️Your barn has a pen for each animal: 🐰 bunny (free), 🐿️ squirrel, 🐸 frog, 🦌 fawn, 🦉 owl and ✨ Spirit Fawn. You can only befriend an animal once its pen is unlocked and has room. Upgrade a pen up to level 5 to hold more and get gifts faster.
  • 🌙Some animals only come out at night, at dawn and dusk, or in the rain. Nap in the hay by the barn to pass time.
  • 🤗Carry your new friend home. Once it trusts you it hops into your arms. Walk back to your farm and press E to settle it in. Press Q to drop it and E to carry it again. Careful: wolves and wild boars may ambush you on the way home.
  • 🏡Spend your gold: upgrade your home from a tent to a cabin, farmhouse and manor for more hearts, more crop plots and faster gifts. Visit the Blacksmith ⚒️ by the well to forge stronger knives and slingshots. Forging needs coins plus enemy drops: 🐺 fur from wolves, 🦴 tusks from boars, 🦡 claws from Burrow bosses, and rare 🦷 fangs, 👑 royal tusks and 🔮 Earth cores from bosses. The Trader swaps spare food for fur, tusks and claws.
  • 🌟Rare animals glow with a coloured ring: 🔷 rare, 🟣 epic, 🌟 legendary. They give more XP, but taming one can wake a Burrow boss that digs out of the ground to take it back. Beat it for burrow claws and, from the King, an Earth core.
  • 🐺Night raids: every night wolf packs march on your farm in waves. They break in through the gates, trample your crops, scare your barn animals and attack you. When one crouches, it's about to pounce. Fight back with your knife (F) or slingshot (G).
  • 🍲Cooking: the pot by the well turns crops and fish into meals. Eat them from your bag to heal hearts and refill stamina, or sell them for more than the ingredients.
  • 🐾Walking buddy: tap "Walk with me" over a barn animal and it follows you around the map. Buddies sniff out treats and warn you when wolves are close.
  • 🎣Fishing: buy a rod at the Shop, stand by the creek and press E to cast. When the bobber dips, stop the hook inside the gold zone. Different fish bite in the morning, at night and in the rain. Sell them at the Merchant.
  • 🌅Morning report: after a night raid you get a summary of what happened and a coin bonus for every raider you beat. Lose nothing for an extra perfect-defence bonus.
  • 🛡️Farm defences: open your home (🏡) to build gates the raiders must break first, watchtowers that shoot them, and spike traps at the gates.
